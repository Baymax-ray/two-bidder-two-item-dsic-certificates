\subsection{A common capacity measure from conditional supports and IC cycles}\label{sec:common-upper}
The stronger upper certificate retains the rational stream coefficients
but changes their integration and their use in the whole-auction bound.
A full-capacity conditional support can replace stream prices on a region
where the other bidder's virtual values have a verified sign. Sparse
long-range incentive cycles make a further compatible reduction.
The common price is specified independently of any finite allocation
range or finite report domain.

\paragraph{A reusable conditional inequality.}
Set $A=2/3$, $b_0=(4-\sqrt2)/3$, $k_0=b_0-A$, $Z=3/4$ and
\[
 \ell(x)=\begin{cases} A,&x<k_0,\\ b_0-x,&k_0<x<A,\end{cases}
 \quad D_0=\{x<A,\ y<\ell(x)\}.
\]
Let $f(x)=3\ell(x)-2$ below $A$ and $f(x)=1$ above $A$,
$F(x)=\int_x^1 f(s)ds$ and $H_Z(y)=4(y-Z)_+$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reusable-Psi}
\begin{split}
 \Psi_1(x,y)&=3(x-A)_++4F(x)\mathbf1_{y>Z},\\
 \Psi_2(x,y)&=3(y-\ell(x))_+\mathbf1_{x<A}+f(x)H_Z(y).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The first term of $\Psi_2$ is zero above $A$. Integration-boundary values
may be chosen Borel. Both densities are bounded and nonnegative. For
example, above $Z$ and below $A$ the second density is
$(1-H_Z)3(y-\ell)+H_Z(3y-2)\ge0$.
The edge-transfer envelope gives the universal identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Psi-identity}
 \langle\Psi,a\rangle-R=3\int_{D_0}u\ge0
\end{equation}
for every full randomized DSIC/IR conditional mechanism, including
unnormalized utility. Equivalently, $\operatorname{div}\Psi=3$ outside
$D_0$ and zero inside, with the required top/right boundary flux.
The menu $\max(0,x-A,y-A,x+y-b_0)$ attains equality and saturates every
positive price, so $\int(\Psi_1+\Psi_2)=R_0=4/9+2\sqrt2/27$.
Unlike the occupied-wing identity, \eqref{eq:Psi-identity} is valid
without freezing the residual's occupation constraints.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the opposite bidder.}
Write $W_0=[0,43/100]^2$, distinct from $W_{\rm wing}$ in the lower
construction. The support $\Psi$ vanishes on $W_0$. For the inherited
stream field $\phi_i$, exact rational Bernstein subdivision on both
radial charts proves
\[
 \phi_{ij}(v_i,v_{-i})\le0\quad\text{if }v_i\in W_0
 \quad(j=1,2),
\]
for every opposing report. The field value at its integrable radial
origin can be set to zero; the origin IR trace remains in the envelope.
This sign condition permits replacing prices according to the opponent's
report while retaining an upper bound on its own complete screening problem.

Add four symmetric, disjoint two-way IC flows to obtain $\phi'_i$.
One flow acts for a fixed opposing box centered at $(3/5,1/5)$, joining
own-report boxes centered at $(2/5,39/40)$ and $(7/20,23/40)$.
All coordinate halfwidths are $3/200$ and the translated-pair flow density
is $1/20$. Forward and reverse flows have equal mass. Bidder exchange
and simultaneous item exchange produce four copies. Their eight profile
boxes avoid every profile with either bidder in $W_0$.
Each weighted inequality is an actual DSIC deviation with the opponent
held fixed; mass balance cancels utilities and introduces no interior sink.
The virtual correction is opposite on the paired boxes.

Define a common nonnegative capacity density, in physical item coordinates,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:common-Pi}
 \Pi'_j(w,v)=\begin{cases}
 0,&w,v\in W_0,\\
 \Psi_j(v),&w\in W_0, v\notin W_0,\\
 \Psi_j(w),&v\in W_0, w\notin W_0,\\
 \max(0,\phi'_{1j},\phi'_{2j}),&w,v\notin W_0.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\begin{theorem}[A common unrestricted charged support]\label{thm:common-support}
For the full class of complete single-bidder DSIC/IR mechanisms with
marginal allocations in $[0,1]^2$, define
$H_i(\Pi')=\sup_{M_i}\{R_i-\langle\Pi',a_i\rangle\}$.
Then $H_1(\Pi')=H_2(\Pi')=0$ and
$\OPT\le\int\sum_j\Pi'_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When the opponent belongs to $W_0$, use \eqref{eq:Psi-identity}.
Outside that opponent region, the stream envelope and the added
nonnegative IC inequalities bound revenue by the $\phi'_i$ allocation
pairing minus nonnegative IC/IR slack. If the own report is in $W_0$,
its virtual coefficients are nonpositive and the cycle correction is zero;
otherwise \eqref{eq:common-Pi} dominates them. Thus
$R_i-\langle\Pi',a_i\rangle\le0$ for every complete $M_i$.
The empty mechanism attains zero. Integrating joint capacity gives the
auction bound. This argument applies directly to jointly measurable
mechanisms with integrable payments and bounded allocations; it never
interchanges an uncountable supremum with an integral. Positive field
parts are integrable, and the absolutely continuous pairings cover the
Lebesgue-measurable class stated in Section 3.
\end{proof}
Solving both charged problems does not produce an equality auction.
For example, empty leaves all positively priced capacity unused.
A matching mechanism must satisfy the capacity and incentive equalities
at the same time.

\paragraph{Which equality conditions remain unsatisfied.}
Put $O_i=\{v_{-i}\in W_0\}$. The exact gap for the measure is the sum
of four nonnegative mechanism slacks,
\begin{align*}
 S_{\rm screen}&=\sum_i3\int_{O_i}\int_{D_0}u_i,\\
 S_{\rm capacity}&=\sum_j\int\Pi'_j(1-a_{1j}-a_{2j}),\\
 S_{\rm virtual}&=\sum_{i,j}\int_{O_i^c}(\Pi'_j-\phi'_{ij})a_{ij},\\
 S_{\rm IC}&=\sum_i\int_{O_i^c}u_i(0;v_{-i})
   +\sum_{\rm cycle\ edges}\int\text{weighted truthful IC residual}.
\end{align*}
They satisfy $\int\Pi'-R=\sum S$. The origin IR trace is explicit;
there is no positive interior sink at every type. Screening's internal
utility terms are not counted again in $S_{\rm IC}$.
The reported rational endpoint additionally includes
\[
 E_{\rm cert}=(B_{20}-B)+(D-\Delta_{1024})\ge0,
 \qquad U_*-R=\sum S+E_{\rm cert}.
\]
Numerical majorization slack is thus separated from mechanism equality.
The archived lottery-box tests show positive slack against the historical
V4.6 and V4.6.1 candidates. Such a test diagnoses the tested certificate;
it does not establish an admissible primal improvement or suboptimality
of a candidate. The fresh audit also checks the selected mechanism on the box centered
at $(151/200,3/40,9/10,63/200)$ with halfwidth $10^{-4}$. Its base fee
is active, but bidder 1 remains uniquely empty and bidder 2 uniquely
chooses $(1,\beta)$, with $9/10<\beta<49/50$. An independently
reconstructed whole-box bound makes its safe virtual value strictly
negative. Consequently the safe capacity-plus-virtual slack is at least
$-\beta\phi'_{2,\rm safe}>0$ there. The exact positive integrated
rational lower bound is sealed in the fresh audit certificate. This
establishes that the common measure does not match the latest mechanism,
without inferring its primal suboptimality.
