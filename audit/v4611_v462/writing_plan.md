# Writing plan and terminology

The auction admits an explicit feasible mechanism with an independently integrated algebraic revenue and a stronger unrestricted common-capacity upper certificate; the two remain unmatched.

Routing: manuscript / research / abstract, introduction, methods-theory, discussion, title / zh-to-en / generic. Primary readers are mechanism-design and mathematical-optimization researchers. Preserve the eight main sections and the historical certificate chain. Draft the new mathematical statements first and derive the abstract from supported results.
