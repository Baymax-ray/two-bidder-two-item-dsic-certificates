# Internal Nature-style self-review and revision

The archive was independently audited and updated before this review round.
Three fresh, mutually blind contexts then reviewed the same frozen 20-page
manuscript and 230-file source packet. Their emphasis briefs were fixed in
advance. All three reports were finalized and hash-frozen before comparison.
They are internal AI-assisted assessments, not external peer review or an
editorial decision.

- [Reviewer 1](reviewer_1.md) emphasized analytic validity and full-class scope.
- [Reviewer 2](reviewer_2.md) emphasized theoretical contribution and readership.
- [Reviewer 3](reviewer_3.md) emphasized the mechanism and exact revenue definition.
- [Post-review synthesis](synthesis.md) compares the frozen findings.
- [Author revision ledger](revision_ledger.md) records implemented changes and remaining limits.

No report identified a Blocking Yes concern. The common major concern was
that the theoretical insight needed a clearer explanation than the decimal
milestone alone. The revisions make the capacity-price versus actual-revenue
derivative distinction explicit, include both bidders' full income effects,
and add a stable mechanism/revenue specification. The revised manuscript is
25 pages and preserves its eight main sections. Broad Nature-level significance
is not certified by this internal process.

`submitted/` retains the exact manuscript and wrapper files seen by reviewers
that changed afterward. Unchanged certificate sources are shared with the
release. `packet_SHA256SUMS` reconstructs the entire original input packet;
`verify_review_snapshot.py` checks every one of its 230 identities without
requiring the original research workspace. `REPORTS_SHA256SUMS` seals exactly
three reports, which have not been rewritten after comparison.

The `joint_explanation_check` files and `reference_index.md` are subsequent
author revision checks, not additional reviewer reports. All source evidence
and claims retain their stated execution boundaries.
