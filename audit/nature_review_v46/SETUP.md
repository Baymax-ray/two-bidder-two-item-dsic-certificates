# Immutable self-review packet

This is an internal AI-assisted pre-submission assessment, not external peer review or an editorial decision. Review the updated 20-page manuscript and its supplied exact proof sources. Evaluate originality, scientific importance, interdisciplinary readership, technical soundness and readability against common_criteria.md. No claim of an exact global optimizer is made by the manuscript.

## Evidence and boundary

Start with archive/manuscript/manuscript.tex and joint_residual_screening.tex, manuscript.pdf and references.bib. archive/README.md summarizes current claims. Certificate source directories provide the mathematical implementations, frozen manifests and proof notes. This packet deliberately omits prior independent audit reports and fresh audit scripts, so it is a source-reading packet, not the supported complete standalone reproduction export. The full archive's public runner uses those omitted audits. Existing transcripts identify computational evidence; a transcript or a hash does not alone replace a theorem or a fresh replay. Do not infer fresh full arithmetic execution unless you actually perform it. No new literature search is required. Literature priority beyond the supplied comparisons may be marked not assessable.

## Common review contract

Produce one report in Chinese with source-faithful formulas and required English field labels where useful. Address all five axes. Include Overall assessment, Who would be interested, Major strengths, Major Concerns, Minor Comments, Technical failings, Nature-style criteria, Recommendation posture, Risk / unsupported claims. Every substantive concern must have a stable reviewer-specific ID, severity, claim pointer and verifiable evidence pointer. Major concerns also require Blocking Yes/No, rationale and a concrete resolution test. Minor comments are never blocking. Do not invent concerns, validation, reviewers, identities or editorial decisions. State when a tier has no supported concerns. Use the skill's technical taxonomy as internal coverage, not a concern quota. Avoid habitual em/en dashes and colon-connected prose. Review only this packet and common skill criteria. Do not open other reviewers' outputs, original conversation, prior audit reports or shared concern ledgers. Freeze your report once written; no comparison-driven edits.
