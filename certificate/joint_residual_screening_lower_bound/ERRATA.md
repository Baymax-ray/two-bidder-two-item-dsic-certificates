# Clarifications to the frozen source notes

The source snapshot preserves the audited historical files byte for byte.
The updated manuscript uses the following corrected statements.

1. Some retained tie rules depend on the shared base allocation and thus on
   both reports. They still choose a utility maximizer from one fixed
   opponent-dependent menu, which proves DSIC. Only the new splices use the
   listed fixed priorities. Do not replace inherited ties by independent
   per-bidder ties. This qualifies the blanket priority sentence in the
   historical V4.6 REPORT.
2. In the lottery proof, D0 is the complement of two strict envelope regions.
   The candidate utility is zero almost everywhere on D0, not at every face.
   At t=7/10 and (x,y)=(2/3,1), its utility is 62347/93750>0, although that
   face belongs to D0. The gap identity uses a volume integral, so the
   corrected statement is sufficient. All line-price and pointwise capacity
   conditions remain separate and unchanged.

Independent audits found no mathematical obstruction to the stated lower
mechanism, exact revenue, conditional supports or strict gap. These are
scope clarifications, not a pointwise repair or an alteration of parameters.
