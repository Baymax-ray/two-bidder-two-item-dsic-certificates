# V4 route ledger

## Kept: eliminate a complete bidder, preserve pointwise capacity

The nesting identity is exact on global Borel mechanisms. Concavity follows from mixing whole mechanisms, and the selected-subgradient representation retains every report. This is now the functional starting point. No finite allocation-range reduction or finite type grid is assumed.

## Successful candidate test: free fibers, then genuine release

The V3 allocation exposes a maximal closed set of wholly free bidder-2 fibers. A strictly interior version supports an exact unrestricted single-buyer solve and a positive exact revenue gain. A second construction changes bidder 1 globally by utility truncation, creating more free fibers, and redesigns bidder 2's menu there. The latter transfers an item from one bidder to the other on positive volume while retaining a strict revenue guarantee above V3. The joint outcome escapes the old containment rule even though the bidder-1 release itself is a deletion operation.

This does not identify the full residual best response outside these fibers. Continuing with the old bidder-2 row elsewhere is a complete feasible extension, not an assertion of full optimization. No alternating-best-response convergence argument is used.

## Successful structural test: future bottlenecks

The exactly solved `(rho(t),0)` face shows how IC transports a pointwise capacity bottleneck to many reports. The binding future envelope gives a forced lottery, its global supporting measure, and a nonlinear fully reoptimized directional formula. The support is extended to arbitrary two-item competitors by a second-item gross-value term, rather than silently restricting the dual to the one-item face.

## Rejected assumptions

- A pointwise residual functional is not a functional of a.e. capacity classes. Null lines and endpoint reports can affect its value.
- A finite countably additive support need not exist at arbitrary Borel residuals; positivity, countable additivity and a strictly positive capacity counterexample show the failure explicitly.
- Homogeneity on a bounded capacity box does not justify an Euler identity for every affine support at the boundary.
- Applying the free-fiber improvement symmetrically to both bidders without checking the joint mechanism is infeasible.
- A valid support need not equal every one-sided directional value change.
- The release guarantee does not prove release beats the zero-release mechanism.

## Next decision point

A further candidate advance requires solving genuinely constrained residual fibers, or constructing an admissible outer change whose fully optimized affected fibers are tractable. A matching certificate requires an inner support at the actual residual and global bidder-1 optimality against that same support. The positive and negative examples delimit that task; they do not establish a universal finite-menu structure. Reopen a route only with a concrete residual geometry, a binding IC transport, or an analytically complete admissible change.
