\subsection{Enlarged conditional support and coupled incentive flows}\label{sec:v5-upper}
We specify the common measure and certify its total mass.
The essential change is to account for the opposing stream field when
enlarging the replacement region; its sign need not remain negative.

Put $J=(43/100,1/2]\times[0,7/20]$ and
$D^\sharp=W_0\cup J\cup J^{\rm swap}$. The reusable support
\eqref{eq:reusable-Psi} and its item swap both vanish on $D^\sharp\subset D_0$.
Let $\sigma(t_1,t_2)=(t_2,t_1)$ and $J^{\rm swap}=\sigma J$.
Set $o_i(w)=\sigma$ on $J^{\rm swap}$ and $o_i(w)=\mathrm{id}$ elsewhere,
with $\Psi^{\mathrm{id}}=\Psi$ and $\Psi^\sigma(t)=\sigma\Psi(\sigma t)$.
Thus $o_i$ depends only on the opponent report and the density is expressed
in physical item coordinates. The no-sale set $D_0$ is invariant under
$\sigma$. For $\theta_i=\mathbf1_{\{v_{-i}\in D^\sharp\}}$, the conditional
identity, valid for almost every opponent report in the stated jointly
measurable class, is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v5-conditional}
 R_i(v_{-i})=\int\big[(1-\theta_i)\phi_i+
                   \theta_i\Psi^{o_i}\big]\cdot a_i
 -(1-\theta_i)u_i(0;v_{-i})-3\theta_i\int_{D_0}u_i.
\end{equation}
The mixing coefficient depends only on the opponent; consequently the
identity introduces no own-type interface derivative. All source terms
are nonnegative under IR, including for unnormalized utilities.

\paragraph{Actual IC inequalities on shared endpoint cells.}
Combine the four sparse cycles with a coupled system of 129 nonnegative
two-way translated IC columns. Column $\ell$ fixes bidder $i$, an opponent
rectangle $J_\ell$, a common own-offset rectangle $K_\ell$, two own centers
$A_\ell,B_\ell$ and a density $\lambda_\ell\ge0$. With
$d_\ell=A_\ell-B_\ell$, the two truthful inequalities give
\[
 K_\ell(a)=\lambda_\ell\int_{K_\ell\times J_\ell}
 d_\ell\cdot[a_i(A_\ell+s,w)-a_i(B_\ell+s,w)]\,ds\,dw\ge0.
\]
The induced allocation coefficients are $+\lambda_\ell d_\ell$ on the
first box and $-\lambda_\ell d_\ell$ on the second. Densities refer to
Lebesgue measure, not normalized probabilities. Every column
admits this direct IC proof; no Hessian-density product or singular
positive-semidefinite representation is needed here.

In the base chart the system has 159 distinct endpoint cells, of which 54 are shared.
On a cell $Q_c$, sum every column correction before taking the itemwise
maximum. If $L_{cjk}\le\max_l\phi_{lj}-\phi_{kj}$ for the old alternatives
$k=0,1,2$ (seller included), then
\[
 \max_k(\phi_{kj}+\delta_{cjk})-\max_k\phi_{kj}
       \le\max_k(\delta_{cjk}-L_{cjk}).
\]
Exact whole-cell bounds therefore certify
\[
 G_{\rm master}=-2\sum_c|Q_c|\sum_j
                   \max_k(\delta_{cjk}-L_{cjk})>0.
\]
The factor two is simultaneous item exchange only. The original and
independent master verifiers reconstruct the cells, shared incidences
and aggregate epigraph. The rational centers, widths, columns and
amplitudes are given in \texttt{master\_certificate.json}
within the portable source closure.

A further translated IC construction uses 64 selected box pairs and
four symmetry copies, hence 512 endpoint boxes. Their entire old and
new virtual winner regions are verified before integration, giving
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v5-BB-gain}
 G_{\rm BB}=\frac{4786305147}{17179869184000000}.
\end{equation}
All added supports avoid profiles with either bidder in $D^\sharp$;
the sparse, master and BB supports are mutually disjoint except for
the explicitly aggregated master endpoints. The independent audit
checks all 130,816 pairwise separations among the BB endpoint boxes.
The correction is a nonnegative sum of actual IC inequalities regardless
of whether those inequalities bind at the proposed mechanism.

Let $\bar\phi$ be the full radial-plus-stream field with these sparse,
master and BB corrections. Define, in physical item coordinates,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v5-Pi}
 \alpha_i=(1-\theta_i)\bar\phi_i+\theta_i\Psi^{o_i},\qquad
 \Pi_j=\max(0,\alpha_{1j},\alpha_{2j}).
\end{equation}
Disjointness ensures that the opponent switch does not cut any IC flow.
Integrating \eqref{eq:v5-conditional} and adding the nonnegative IC
terms proves $\operatorname{Rev}_i(M_i)\le\langle\Pi,a_i\rangle$ for each complete
DSIC/IR mechanism. Thus both unrestricted charged values are zero
(empty attains zero), and joint capacity gives $\OPT\le\int\sum_j\Pi_j$.
This proof applies to arbitrary randomized allocations in the stated
measurable class; it places no finite-menu restriction on competitors.

\paragraph{Paying the opposing field on the enlarged region.}
For $w\in J$, write $a=\phi_j(v;w)$, $b=\phi_j(w;v)$ and
$p=\Psi_j(v)\ge0$. On a single-replacement profile,
\[
 \max(0,a,b)-\max(p,b)
 \ge a_+-p-(b-p)_+,
\]
because $\max(p,b)=p+(b-p)_+$ and the old maximum is at least $a_+$.
On double-replacement profiles the two directed
bounds cancel, and the old nonnegative price covers them. On $W_0$
the extra directed contribution is nonpositive and remains a valid
lower bound. Consequently the complete splice improvement satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v5-splice-gain}
 G_{\rm splice}\ge4\left[L_- -\frac{49}{2000}R_{{\rm SB},+}-E_+\right],
\end{equation}
where $L=\int_J\int\sum_j(\phi_j(v;w))_+$ and
$E=\int_J\int\sum_j(\phi_j(w;v)-\Psi_j(v))_+$.
The subscripts denote directed bounds, so the right side is rational.
The factor four counts the two bidder directions and the two oriented
added rectangles $J,J^{\rm swap}$. The item sum is already inside $L$
and $E$, so it contributes no further multiplicity. The low-square and
double-replacement cases are expanded in Lemma~\ref{lem:overlap} of Appendix~\ref{app:overlap}.
The certificate obtains
\[
 L_-=\frac{119448693432877}{8796093022208000},\qquad
 E_+<0.000011750019244,
\]
yielding a guaranteed reduction exceeding $0.00045027837569$. Opposing excess and overlap are both included.

The $L$ calculation uses continuous Jensen bounds on 49 opponent cells
and $128^2$ own-chart cells. The $E$ certificate covers 294 roots with
12,000 splits and 12,294 leaves. Its interval arithmetic starts with
exact rational input brackets and expands every binary64 operation
outward using \texttt{nextafter}; final dyadic bounds are accumulated
with integers. IEEE binary64 round-to-nearest semantics and NumPy's
\texttt{nextafter} are therefore additional trusted assumptions.
The full tree was freshly replayed. The separate splice audit rebuilds
the identity and selected exact checks but reuses the primary tree
implementation; it is not a second independently implemented full tree.

\paragraph{The exact endpoint and its remaining slack.}
The reference enclosure remainder is
$E_*=(B_{20}-B)+(D-\Delta_{1024})\ge0$.
A fresh fixed-point traversal at depths $(20,20,26)$ recovers a rational
lower bound $E_-\le E_*$, approximately $0.00012090110466538207$.
This contracts numerical enclosure; it is not another decrease in the
actual measure. With the rational lower bound in
\eqref{eq:v5-splice-gain} denoted by $G_{{\rm splice},-}$, set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v5-upper}
 U=U_{\rm ref}-E_- -G_{\rm master}-G_{{\rm splice},-}-G_{\rm BB}.

Every rational is exported in the final manifest and checked by separate
