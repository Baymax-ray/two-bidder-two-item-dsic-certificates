\paragraph{A reusable conditional inequality.}
Set $A=2/3$, $b_0=(4-\sqrt2)/3$, $k_0=b_0-A$, $Z=3/4$ and
\[
 \ell(x)=\begin{cases}
 A,&0\le x\le k_0,\\
 b_0-x,&k_0<x<A,\\
 k_0,&A\le x\le1,
 \end{cases}
 \quad D_0=\{x<A,\ y<\ell(x)\}.
\]
Let $f(x)=3\ell(x)-2$ for $0\le x<A$ and $f(x)=1$ for $A\le x\le1$,
$F(x)=\int_x^1 f(s)ds$ and $H_Z(y)=4(y-Z)_+$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reusable-Psi}
\begin{split}
 \Psi_1(x,y)&=3(x-A)_++4F(x)\mathbf1_{y>Z},\\
 \Psi_2(x,y)&=3(y-\ell(x))_+\mathbf1_{x<A}+f(x)H_Z(y).
\end{split}
\end{equation}
The first term of $\Psi_2$ is zero for $x\ge A$. These formulas fix a
Borel version on all boundaries. Both densities are bounded and nonnegative. For
example, above $Z$ and below $A$ the second density is
$(1-H_Z)3(y-\ell)+H_Z(3y-2)\ge0$.
The edge-transfer envelope gives the universal identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Psi-identity}
 \langle\Psi,a\rangle-R=3\int_{D_0}u\ge0
\end{equation}
for every measurable, integrable randomized DSIC/IR conditional mechanism,
including unnormalized utility. Applied to a complete jointly measurable
mechanism, it holds on almost every opponent fiber and yields the integrated
inequality by Fubini. Equivalently, $\operatorname{div}\Psi=3$ outside
$D_0$ and zero inside, with the required top/right boundary flux.
The menu $\max(0,x-A,y-A,x+y-b_0)$ attains equality and saturates every
positive price, so $\int(\Psi_1+\Psi_2)=R_{\rm SB}=4/9+2\sqrt2/27$.
Identity~\eqref{eq:Psi-identity} holds without imposing
occupation constraints on the residual.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the opposite bidder.}
Write $W_0=[0,43/100]^2$, for the support switch. The support $\Psi$ vanishes on $W_0$. For the specified
stream field $\phi_i$, exact rational Bernstein subdivision on both
radial charts proves
\[
 \phi_{ij}(v_i,v_{-i})\le0\quad\text{if }v_i\in W_0
 \quad(j=1,2),
\]
for every opposing report. The field value at its integrable radial
origin can be set to zero; the origin IR trace remains in the envelope.
This sign condition permits replacing prices according to the opponent's
report while retaining an upper bound on its own complete screening problem.

Add four symmetric, disjoint two-way IC flows to obtain $\phi'_i$.
One flow acts for a fixed opposing box centered at $(3/5,1/5)$, joining
own-report boxes centered at $(2/5,39/40)$ and $(7/20,23/40)$.
All coordinate halfwidths are $3/200$ and the translated-pair flow density
is $1/20$. Forward and reverse flows have equal mass. Bidder exchange
and simultaneous item exchange produce four copies. Their eight profile
boxes avoid every profile with either bidder in $W_0$.
Each weighted inequality is an actual DSIC deviation with the opponent
held fixed; mass balance cancels utilities and introduces no interior sink.
The virtual correction is opposite on the paired boxes.

Define a common nonnegative capacity density, in physical item coordinates,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:common-Pi}
 \Pi'_j(w,v)=\begin{cases}
 0,&w,v\in W_0,\\
 \Psi_j(v),&w\in W_0, v\notin W_0,\\
 \Psi_j(w),&v\in W_0, w\notin W_0,\\
 \max(0,\phi'_{1j},\phi'_{2j}),&w,v\notin W_0.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
\begin{theorem}[A common unrestricted charged support]\label{thm:common-support}
For the full class of complete single-bidder DSIC/IR mechanisms with
marginal allocations in $[0,1]^2$, define
\[
H_i(\Pi)=\sup_{M_i}\{\operatorname{Rev}_i(M_i)-\langle\Pi,a_i\rangle\}.
\]
Then $H_1(\Pi')=H_2(\Pi')=0$ and
$\OPT\le\int\sum_j\Pi'_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
When the opponent belongs to $W_0$, use \eqref{eq:Psi-identity}.
Outside that opponent region, the stream envelope and the added
nonnegative IC inequalities bound revenue by the $\phi'_i$ allocation
pairing minus nonnegative IC/IR slack. If the own report is in $W_0$,
its virtual coefficients are nonpositive and the cycle correction is zero;
otherwise \eqref{eq:common-Pi} dominates them. Thus
$\operatorname{Rev}_i(M_i)-\langle\Pi',a_i\rangle\le0$ for every complete $M_i$.
The empty mechanism attains zero. Integrating joint capacity gives the
auction bound. This argument applies directly to jointly measurable
mechanisms with integrable payments and bounded allocations; it never
interchanges an uncountable supremum with an integral. Positive field
parts are integrable, and the absolutely continuous pairings cover the
Lebesgue-measurable class stated in Section~\ref{sec:model}.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Common price and its mass.}
The field $\phi_i=F_i$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:witness} already defines
a common density $\Pi^0_j=\max(0,\phi_{1j},\phi_{2j})$. The stream identity gives
\[
 \operatorname{Rev}_i(M_i)=\int\sum_j\phi_{ij}a_{ij}-\int u_i(0;v_{-i})
 \le\langle\Pi^0,a_i\rangle,
\]
and the empty mechanism attains equality. Hence
$H_1(\Pi^0)=H_2(\Pi^0)=0$ as well. Existence of a common measure and
zero charged values are already consequences of the specified stream
argument. The conditional replacements and compatible IC cycles below
instead prove a strict decrease in its total mass,
$\int\Pi'<\int\Pi^0$, while preserving validity for both unrestricted
conditional problems. This integrated comparison does not assert a
pointwise price decrease.

Solving both charged problems does not produce an equality auction.
For example, empty leaves all positively priced capacity unused.
A matching mechanism must satisfy the capacity and incentive equalities
at the same time.

\subsection{The overlap-safe support splice}\label{app:overlap}
Let $K=J\cup J^{\rm swap}$, $D^\sharp=W_0\cup K$, and let $\Pi^W$
be the common price with only the $W_0$ switch. The added rectangles satisfy
$\max(w)\le1/2$ and $\sum_jw_j\le17/20<b_0$; also $2(43/100)<b_0$.
Thus both orientations of $\Psi$ vanish on $D^\sharp\subset D_0$.
All IC corrections used in the final certificate avoid profiles where
either bidder belongs to $D^\sharp$.

\begin{lemma}[Complete splice decrement]\label{lem:overlap}
For $L,E$ and $J$ as in \eqref{eq:v5-splice-gain},
\[
 \int\sum_j(\Pi^W_j-\Pi_j)
 \ge4\left[L-|J|R_{\rm SB}-E\right],\qquad |J|=49/2000,
\]
with the same inequality after adding the disjoint IC corrections.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For one oriented added opponent $w\in J$, set
$a=\phi_j(v;w)$, $b=\phi_j(w;v)$ and $p=\Psi_j(v)\ge0$.
If $v\notin D^\sharp$, the prices are $\max(0,a,b)$ and
$\max(p,b)$, so their difference is at least
$a_+-p-(b-p)_+$.

If $v\in W_0$, both actual prices are zero. The low-square sign
certificate gives $a\le0$, and both supports vanish. The directed
expression equals $-b_+\le0$, so it is still a valid lower bound.

If both reports belong to $K$, both replacement supports vanish.
The two directed expressions, using the respective support orientations,
are $a_+-b_+$ and $b_+-a_+$. Their sum is zero, while the actual
old price is nonnegative and the new price is zero. The two replacements
are therefore charged together, without assuming disjoint bidder events.

If neither report belongs to $K$, neither the price nor the directed sum
changes. These cases exhaust the report space, including cross-orientation
overlaps. Integration of each directed expression gives
$L-|J|R_{\rm SB}-E$. Bidder exchange and the two oriented rectangles give
factor four; the item sum is already inside $L$ and $E$. Disjoint IC
supports are unaffected by every switch, so their certified decrements
add separately. This proves the claim.
\end{proof}
